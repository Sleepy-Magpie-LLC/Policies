**RekiBase**

**Privacy Policy**

*Effective Date: April 1, 2026*

Thank you for using RekiBase. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information RekiBase collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

# **1\. Overview**

RekiBase is designed with privacy at its core. The app does not transmit your personal data to external servers, does not sell your data, and does not share it with third parties, with the limited exception described in Section 2.3 (Location Data) and summarized in Section 4 (Data Sharing).

# **2\. Data We Collect**

## **2.1 Data You Choose to Store**

RekiBase allows you to optionally store personal information (such as names, email addresses, or other contact details) within the app. This is entirely at your discretion. All such data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted off your device or shared with any third party.

## **2.2 In-App Purchases**

When you upgrade to RekiBase Pro via an in-app purchase (e.g., Google Play, Apple App Store), the transaction is processed entirely by the platform provider (Google or Apple). RekiBase does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information. Please refer to the relevant platform's privacy policy for details:

* Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

* Apple App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## **2.3 Location Data**

If you grant location permission, RekiBase may access your device's location solely when you perform a location search within the app. Your location is sent to the Google Places API to retrieve nearby places relevant to your search.

Important details about location data:

* Location is only accessed when you actively perform a search.

* Location data is not stored by RekiBase on any server.

* Location data is not shared with any party other than Google Places API to fulfill your search request.

* You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

Google's handling of location data sent to the Places API is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## **2.4 Automatically Collected Information**

RekiBase does not automatically collect or transmit any non-personal data, such as crash logs, usage statistics, device identifiers (like IP address or device model), or metadata, other than the temporary transmission of location data to the Google Places API when you initiate a search, as detailed in Section 2.3.

# **3\. Local Data Storage**

All data created or stored within RekiBase is saved locally on your device. RekiBase does not operate a cloud sync service or backup system.

You are solely responsible for:

* Backing up your data.

* Maintaining the integrity of your data.

* Deleting your data when no longer needed.

If you uninstall the app or your device is lost, damaged, or reset, your locally stored data may be permanently lost. RekiBase is not liable for any data loss.

# **4\. Data Sharing**

RekiBase does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, with the following narrow exception:

* Google Places API: Your device location is shared only when you initiate a location search, solely to return relevant nearby results.

No other data is shared externally under any circumstances.

# **5\. Children's Privacy**

RekiBase is not directed at children under the age of 13\. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

# **6\. Platform Availability**

RekiBase is currently available on Android and iOS. Desktop versions may become available in the future. This Privacy Policy applies to all platforms on which RekiBase is offered. We will update this policy if new platform-specific data practices are introduced.

# **7\. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of RekiBase after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

# **9\. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)**

We respect your privacy rights and recognize that residents of certain jurisdictions have additional rights concerning their personal data. **Right to Know and Delete (CCPA):** RekiBase does not sell personal information. Because all personal data you choose to store is saved locally on your device, we are unable to fulfill requests for access, correction, or deletion of this data on your behalf. To exercise these rights, you must manage the data directly within the app on your device (e.g., viewing, modifying, or deleting stored entries). **GDPR Rights:** Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) have rights including the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data. Since the app is built on local storage, you control the data on your device. Any location data sent to the Google Places API is immediately processed and not stored by RekiBase. For questions regarding the limited data we process (as defined in Section 2.3), please contact us using the details in Section 10\.
