**Morse Trainer**

**Privacy Policy**

*Effective Date: October 24, 2026*

Thank you for using Morse Trainer. This Privacy Policy explains what information Morse Trainer handles, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

# **1\. Overview**

Morse Trainer works entirely offline. The app does not collect personal information, does not connect to the internet, does not transmit any data off your device, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

# **2\. Data We Collect**

We do not collect any data. Morse Trainer has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting.

## **2.1 Data Stored on Your Device**

To work, Morse Trainer saves a small amount of information locally on your device:

* Your training progress, such as unlocked lesson characters, recent attempts, and per-character accuracy and response times.

* Your app settings, such as speed (WPM), tone pitch, output mode, and vibration options.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

## **2.2 Permissions**

On Android, Morse Trainer requests only the vibration permission, which it uses to play Morse code as vibration and to give feedback while keying. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or network.

## **2.3 Automatically Collected Information**

Morse Trainer does not automatically collect or transmit any information, including crash logs, usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or metadata.

# **3\. Local Data Storage**

All data created by Morse Trainer is saved locally on your device:

* **Android:** in the app's private storage. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app, so this data is not copied to Google's backup service.

* **macOS:** in `~/Library/Application Support/MorseTrainer/`.

Morse Trainer does not operate a cloud sync or backup service. If you uninstall the app, or your device is lost, damaged, or reset, your progress and settings may be permanently lost. You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app (Android) or deleting the folder above (macOS).

# **4\. Data Sharing**

Morse Trainer does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

# **5\. Children's Privacy**

Morse Trainer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you have questions about this, please contact us using the details in Section 9.

# **6\. Platform Availability**

Morse Trainer is currently available on Android and macOS. This Privacy Policy applies to all platforms on which Morse Trainer is offered. We will update this policy if new platform-specific data practices are introduced.

# **7\. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Morse Trainer after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

# **8\. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)**

We respect your privacy rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Morse Trainer does not sell personal information and does not collect or process personal data on our systems. Because everything the app stores stays on your device, we hold no data about you to access, correct, or delete. You remain in full control and can delete your data directly on your device as described in Section 3.
